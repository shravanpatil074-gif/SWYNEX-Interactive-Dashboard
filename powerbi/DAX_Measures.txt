SWYNEX Task 3 - Power BI DAX Measures

Table name: cleaned_titanic_dashboard

Total Passengers =
COUNTROWS(cleaned_titanic_dashboard)

Survivors =
CALCULATE(
    COUNTROWS(cleaned_titanic_dashboard),
    cleaned_titanic_dashboard[Survived] = 1
)

Survival Rate =
DIVIDE([Survivors], [Total Passengers], 0)

Average Age =
AVERAGE(cleaned_titanic_dashboard[Age])

Average Fare =
AVERAGE(cleaned_titanic_dashboard[Fare])

Average Family Size =
AVERAGE(cleaned_titanic_dashboard[Family_Size])

Formatting:
- Survival Rate: Percentage, 2 decimals
- Average Age: Decimal number, 2 decimals
- Average Fare: Decimal number, 2 decimals
- Average Family Size: Decimal number, 2 decimals
